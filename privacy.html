Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026  ·  Effective: June 1, 2026

smbautomation.ai ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website at smbautomation.ai or contact us about our services.

If you are a California resident, please see the California Privacy Rights section below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you:

  • Submit a contact or booking form (name, business name, phone number)
  • Submit the ROI calculator email form (email address)
  • Call or email us directly
  • Communicate with us by any other means

We may also automatically collect certain information when you visit our website, including:

  • Log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent)
  • Device information (operating system, screen size)
  • Referral source (how you found us)
  • Cookie and usage data (see Section 5 below)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Contact you about your inquiry and schedule consultations
  • Send you information you requested (e.g., the ROI report or 5 Automations guide)
  • Provide, operate, and improve our services
  • Respond to your comments and questions
  • Send you marketing communications (only where you have consented)
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Text Message and Phone Communications (TCPA)

By providing your phone number and submitting a form on our website, you expressly consent to receive calls and text messages from smbautomation.ai related to your inquiry, including automated or pre-recorded messages. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message or by contacting us at hello@smbautomation.ai.

We comply with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and do not send unsolicited commercial text messages.

4. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with the following third-party service providers who assist us in operating our business:

  • GoHighLevel — CRM and marketing automation platform (stores contact info and manages follow-up communications)
  • Synthflow — AI voice agent platform (processes inbound call data)
  • Twilio — Communications platform (routes phone calls and SMS)
  • Google Analytics — Website analytics (collects anonymized usage data)
  • Netlify — Web hosting and content delivery

Each of these providers is contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We may also disclose your information if required by law, legal process, or government request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of smbautomation.ai, our clients, or the public.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience. These include:

  • Essential cookies — Required for the website to function properly (e.g., session management)
  • Analytics cookies — Used by Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Data is aggregated and anonymized. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When your information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know — You may request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the purposes for collection, and the third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to Delete — You may request that we delete personal information we have collected about you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Correct — You may request that we correct inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale — We do not sell personal information. If this changes, we will update this policy and provide an opt-out mechanism.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise your California privacy rights, contact us at hello@smbautomation.ai with "California Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 45 days.

Categories of personal information collected in the past 12 months: Identifiers (name, email, phone number, IP address), commercial information (services of interest), internet activity (browsing and interaction data on our site).

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

10. Links to Third-Party Sites
