How much does an answering service cost in 2026?

Real pricing for answering services, virtual receptionists, and AI — plus the hidden per-minute fees nobody quotes up front.

By Wiley Strahan/7 min read/Updated May 2026

"How much does an answering service cost?" has an annoying answer: it depends — and the way most providers price it makes a real comparison hard on purpose. Here is what you will actually pay in 2026, including the fees nobody volunteers up front.

The short version

  • Traditional answering services bill per minute, so your cost rises exactly when you are busiest.
  • Virtual receptionists cost more but do more than take messages.
  • AI answering is typically flat-rate from around $297/mo with no per-minute meter.

Traditional answering services: per-minute pricing

Most legacy answering services bill by the minute, usually sold in monthly bundles:

Plan sizeIncluded minutesTypical monthly cost
Starter~100 min$120–$250
Mid~250 min$300–$600
Busy~500+ min$700–$1,500

The trap is the overage rate. Go past your bundle and you pay $1.00–$2.00+ per extra minute. So your bill spikes during a storm, a heat wave, or a campaign — precisely when you can least afford surprises.

The hidden fees to ask about

  • Per-minute rounding. Many round up to the next full minute per call. Lots of short calls quietly inflate the bill.
  • Setup and account fees. One-time onboarding charges are common.
  • Holiday and after-hours premiums. Higher rates exactly when you need coverage most.
  • Per-message or per-transfer fees. Some charge for each message taken or call patched through.

Virtual receptionists: more capability, higher price

A trained virtual receptionist who books appointments and screens leads typically runs $600 to $2,500/mo, depending on hours and volume. You are paying for judgment and a warmer experience — worth it for some businesses, overkill for others.

AI answering: flat-rate, no meter

AI receptionists changed the pricing model. Instead of a per-minute meter, you pay a flat monthly rate — typically from around $297/mo — for unlimited answering, booking, and lead capture, 24/7. A storm-day surge of 50 calls costs the same as a quiet Tuesday.

The real cost of an answering service is not the monthly fee. It is the per-minute meter running during your busiest week.

So what should you budget?

  • Low volume, message-taking only: $120–$300/mo on a traditional service.
  • Higher volume or you want booking: AI answering from $297/mo flat is usually the best value.
  • Complex, relationship-heavy calls: $600–$2,500/mo for a virtual receptionist.

The cleanest way to compare is to ignore the headline number and ask one question: what does this cost me on my busiest day? For per-minute services the honest answer is "more." For flat-rate AI answering, it is "the same." See our pricing for exact numbers, or compare the three options head to head.
